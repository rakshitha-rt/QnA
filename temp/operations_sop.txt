PAGE 1 — SECTION 1: SHIPMENT ESCALATION PROCESS FOR DELAYED SHIPMENTS
================================================================================

1.1 PURPOSE
-----------
This section defines the escalation process when a shipment is delayed beyond
the contracted Service Level Agreement (SLA). Timely escalation prevents
revenue loss, customer churn, and supply chain disruption.

1.2 SCOPE
---------
Applies to all outbound and inbound shipments managed by ACME Logistics across
all regional branches: Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, Hyderabad, and Pune.

1.3 DEFINITIONS
---------------
- On-Time Delivery (OTD): Shipment received by the customer within the agreed
  delivery window.
- Delay Threshold Level 1: Shipment late by 1–24 hours.
- Delay Threshold Level 2: Shipment late by 24–72 hours.
- Delay Threshold Level 3: Shipment late by more than 72 hours.
- ETA: Estimated Time of Arrival.

1.4 ESCALATION TIERS AND RESPONSE TIMELINE
-------------------------------------------

LEVEL 1 — MINOR DELAY (1–24 hours)
  Responsible Party : Field Operations Agent
  Action Required   : Update ETA in the tracking system within 2 hours of
                      detecting the delay. Notify the customer via automated
                      SMS/email. Log reason code in the Shipment Management
                      System (SMS Portal).
  Escalation Trigger: If delay is not resolved within 12 hours → escalate to
                      Level 2.

LEVEL 2 — MODERATE DELAY (24–72 hours)
  Responsible Party : Regional Operations Manager
  Action Required   : Contact the carrier directly and obtain a confirmed
                      revised ETA. Send a personal update to the customer
                      account manager. Initiate a re-routing assessment if
                      applicable. Document all actions in the SMS Portal under
                      "Escalation Log."
  Escalation Trigger: If delay is not resolved within 48 hours → escalate to
                      Level 3.

LEVEL 3 — CRITICAL DELAY (72+ hours)
  Responsible Party : VP of Operations + Customer Success Lead
  Action Required   : Convene a bridge call with the customer within 4 hours.
                      Evaluate alternative fulfillment options (nearest hub
                      stock, cross-dock, expedited air freight).
                      Issue a formal Service Credit Note if OTD breach is
                      confirmed. File an internal incident report (IR-OPS).
  Regulatory Note   : If shipment contains regulated goods (pharma, hazmat),
                      notify the Compliance team immediately regardless of
                      delay tier.

1.5 ESCALATION FLOWCHART (TEXT REPRESENTATION)
-----------------------------------------------
  Shipment Delayed?
       |
       ├─ NO  → Continue monitoring, update ETA in system
       |
       └─ YES
            |
            ├─ 1–24 hrs → Field Agent updates ETA, notifies customer (L1)
            |        └─ Unresolved 12 hrs → escalate to L2
            |
            ├─ 24–72 hrs → Ops Manager contacts carrier, re-routes if needed (L2)
            |         └─ Unresolved 48 hrs → escalate to L3
            |
            └─ 72+ hrs → VP Ops + CS Lead, bridge call, credit note (L3)

1.6 DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------
All escalation actions must be logged in the SMS Portal with:
  - Shipment ID
  - Delay tier triggered
  - Root cause (weather, carrier failure, customs hold, etc.)
  - Actions taken and timestamps
  - Resolution date and time

PAGE 2 — SECTION 2: PROCUREMENT APPROVAL WORKFLOW
================================================================================

2.1 PURPOSE
-----------
This section outlines the end-to-end approval workflow for all procurement
requests, ensuring financial control, vendor compliance, and audit readiness.

2.2 PROCUREMENT TIERS
---------------------
All procurement requests are classified by value to determine the approval path:

  Tier A — Up to ₹50,000     : Department Head approval only
  Tier B — ₹50,001–₹2,00,000 : Department Head + Finance Manager approval
  Tier C — ₹2,00,001–₹10,00,000 : Department Head + Finance Manager + CFO
  Tier D — Above ₹10,00,000  : Full committee review (CFO + MD + Legal)

2.3 STANDARD APPROVAL STEPS
-----------------------------
Step 1 — INITIATION
  The requesting department raises a Purchase Request (PR) in the Procurement
  Portal. Required fields: item description, quantity, estimated cost, vendor
  name (or "open tender"), business justification, and required-by date.

Step 2 — DEPARTMENTAL REVIEW (All Tiers)
  Department Head reviews the request within 2 business days. They verify
  budget availability against the approved annual budget. If budget is
  exceeded, the request must include a budget deviation form (BDF-001).

Step 3 — FINANCE REVIEW (Tier B, C, D)
  Finance Manager validates the cost estimate, checks vendor payment terms,
  and confirms PO number allocation. Turnaround time: 3 business days.

Step 4 — CFO APPROVAL (Tier C, D)
  CFO reviews strategic alignment and cash flow impact. Turnaround: 2 business
  days. CFO may request vendor comparison quotes (minimum 3 quotes for Tier C+).

Step 5 — COMMITTEE REVIEW (Tier D only)
  Full committee convenes within 5 business days. Minutes must be recorded.
  Legal reviews contract terms before final sign-off.

Step 6 — PURCHASE ORDER ISSUANCE
  Upon final approval, the Procurement team issues the official PO via the
  Procurement Portal. Vendor is notified by email with PO attachment.

Step 7 — GOODS RECEIPT AND THREE-WAY MATCH
  On delivery, the warehouse team records Goods Receipt Note (GRN). Finance
  performs a three-way match: PO vs. GRN vs. Vendor Invoice before payment.

2.4 EMERGENCY PROCUREMENT
--------------------------
For operational emergencies (e.g., critical spare parts, stoppage situations):
  - Maximum value without prior approval: ₹25,000
  - Retrospective approval must be obtained within 24 hours
  - Emergency PR form (EPR-001) must be filed with root cause explanation
  - More than 3 emergency PRs per quarter triggers a process review

2.5 VENDOR EMPANELMENT REQUIREMENT
------------------------------------
All vendors must be empanelled in the Vendor Master before a PO can be raised.
New vendor onboarding takes 5–7 business days (KYC, bank details, GST verification).

PAGE 3 — SECTION 3: INVENTORY MANAGEMENT & KPI DEFINITIONS
================================================================================

3.1 PURPOSE
-----------
This section defines the key inventory KPIs tracked by ACME Logistics and
provides guidance on data interpretation for operational and management use.

3.2 INVENTORY AGING KPI
------------------------
Definition:
  Inventory Aging measures the number of days a specific Stock Keeping Unit
  (SKU) has been held in the warehouse without being sold or dispatched.

Formula:
  Aging Days = Current Date − Date of Stock Receipt

Classification:
  Fresh Stock   : 0–30 days
  Aging Stock   : 31–60 days     (flag for review)
  At-Risk Stock : 61–90 days     (require markdown or transfer proposal)
  Dead Stock    : 90+ days       (escalate to Inventory Review Committee)

Why It Matters:
  High aging stock ties up working capital, occupies warehouse space, and risks
  obsolescence. The Inventory Review Committee meets monthly to review all
  SKUs with aging > 60 days.

Target Benchmark:
  Average aging days across all active SKUs should remain below 45 days.
  Any branch with average aging > 60 days triggers an automatic audit.

3.3 BRANCH SALES PERFORMANCE KPI
----------------------------------
Definition:
  Monthly gross sales value per branch, measured in INR.

Target: Each branch is assigned a monthly sales target at the start of each
financial year. Performance is tracked weekly and reported in the monthly
Branch Operations Report (BOR).

3.4 FILL RATE KPI
-----------------
Definition:
  Fill Rate = (Orders Fully Shipped / Total Orders Received) × 100

Target: ≥ 95% fill rate per branch per month.
A fill rate below 90% for two consecutive months requires a root cause
analysis and corrective action plan submission.

3.5 STOCK TURN RATIO
---------------------
Definition:
  Stock Turn = (Cost of Goods Sold / Average Inventory Value) × 12 (annualized)

Target: Minimum stock turn of 6× per year for fast-moving categories.
Slow-moving SKUs (turn < 2×) are reviewed for discontinuation.

3.6 REPORTING CADENCE
----------------------
  Daily   : Field agents submit end-of-day stock counts via mobile app.
  Weekly  : Branch Managers review aging and fill rate dashboards.
  Monthly : Branch Operations Report (BOR) submitted by 5th of following month.
  Quarterly: Full inventory audit conducted by Finance + Ops jointly.

PAGE 4 — SECTION 4: GUARDRAILS, EXCEPTIONS, AND CONTACT DIRECTORY
================================================================================

4.1 ESCALATION GUARDRAILS
--------------------------
The following conditions automatically freeze a shipment and trigger immediate
compliance notification:

  a) Shipment involving controlled substances or pharma products held at
     customs for > 12 hours.
  b) Any shipment flagged with a mismatch between declared weight and actual
     weight > 5%.
  c) Customer credit limit exceeded at time of dispatch clearance.
  d) Carrier blacklisted in the Approved Vendor List (AVL) used for dispatch.

In all above cases: DO NOT release the shipment. Notify the Compliance team
at compliance@acmelogistics.in within 1 hour.

4.2 PROCUREMENT EXCEPTIONS
----------------------------
The following are NOT subject to standard approval workflows:
  - Utility bill payments and statutory dues (handled by Finance directly)
  - Petty cash expenses up to ₹5,000 per transaction (submit via Expense Report)
  - Rental renewals below 5% increase and within existing vendor empanelment

4.3 SENSITIVE DATA HANDLING
-----------------------------
All documents containing vendor pricing, contract terms, or customer PII must:
  - Be stored in the secure DMS (Document Management System) with restricted
    access (need-to-know basis only).
  - Not be shared externally without written approval from the Legal team.
  - Be watermarked before sharing with external parties.
